# Where the pictures in the screenshots come from

The screenshots in this repository show a **made-up demo library**. The artists, albums, songs, lyrics and biographies were invented for this page and have nothing to do with the painters and photographers below. The titles and names on the covers were set over the pictures for the screenshots.

## Terms

- **CC0:** the Art Institute of Chicago publishes its public-domain artworks under [Creative Commons Zero](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images). No permission or credit is required; it's given here anyway.
- **NASA media:** NASA's images are [generally not subject to copyright in the United States](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Hubble's Carina Nebula picture is credited as NASA gives it, together with ESA and the team at STScI.

Neither the Art Institute of Chicago nor NASA is affiliated with Songsta or endorses it.
